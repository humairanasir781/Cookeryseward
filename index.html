Our Story

Where Alaska Meets
Artful Dining

The Cookery is a standout dining destination in Seward, known for its refined seafood dishes, curated wine selection, and expertly crafted small plates. Every dish is thoughtfully prepared, balancing flavor, texture, and presentation.

We celebrate the bounty of Alaskan waters with a menu that evolves with the seasons — always fresh, always intentional, always exceptional.
